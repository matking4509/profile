About Mat King

Rooted in a strong Midwestern work ethic with technical foundations built right in Kansas City, Mat believes in a few simple rules: work hard, fix what’s broken, and don't overcomplicate things. Over a 25-plus-year career keeping the digital gears turning for major global companies, he has skipped the flashy corporate buzzwords to focus on practical results—making massive systems reliable, automating the tedious stuff, and cutting out millions in operational waste.

Currently leading large-scale technology operations out of Atlanta, he manages multi-million dollar budgets with common-sense pragmatism, but his real focus is always on the people. He excels at dismantling corporate silos, looking out for his crew, and mentoring the next generation of engineers.

Backed by an MBA focused on Leadership and Teaming, Mat operates on the belief that a technical blueprint is only as good as the team standing behind it. When he isn't keeping enterprise platforms online, he’s usually drawing valuable problem-solving lessons from his diverse hands-on passions—whether he's bringing the structured precision of his culinary training to the kitchen, navigating the focus of scuba diving, or shaping raw materials at the blacksmithing forge.
